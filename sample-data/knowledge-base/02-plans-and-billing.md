# Plans and Billing

TaskFlow is a fictional demonstration SaaS product with Starter, Pro, and Business plans. All listed prices are per user per month.

## Plan Comparison

| Feature | Starter | Pro | Business |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly price | $12 per user | $24 per user | $39 per user |
| Team members | Maximum 10 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Active projects | Maximum 5 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Workspace storage | 5 GB | 100 GB | 500 GB |
| Support | Email support | Priority email support | Priority email and priority chat support |
| Two-factor authentication | Included | Included | Included |
| Custom fields | Not included | Included | Included |
| API access | Not included | Included | Included |
| Slack, Google Drive, and GitHub integrations | Not included | Included | Included |
| SAML single sign-on | Not included | Not included | Included |
| Audit logs | Not included | Not included | Included |
| Advanced user roles | Not included | Not included | Included |

## Starter Plan

Starter costs **$12 per user per month**. It supports up to 10 team members, up to 5 active projects, and 5 GB of workspace storage. It includes email support and two-factor authentication. Starter does not include custom fields, API access, Slack, Google Drive, or GitHub integrations.

## Pro Plan

Pro costs **$24 per user per month**. It supports unlimited team members, unlimited active projects, and 100 GB of workspace storage. It includes custom fields, API access, Slack, Google Drive, and GitHub integrations, priority email support, and two-factor authentication.

## Business Plan

Business costs **$39 per user per month**. It includes everything in Pro, including priority email support and two-factor authentication. It increases workspace storage to 500 GB and adds SAML single sign-on, audit logs, advanced user roles, and priority chat support.

## Monthly and Annual Billing

Monthly and annual billing are available. Annual billing provides a **20 percent discount**. The approved product information does not define annual totals, taxes, invoice dates, proration, or the currency represented by the dollar symbol. Contact billing support for details not stated here.

## Free Trial

A **14-day free trial** is available without a credit card. Conversion steps, trial extensions, and post-trial behavior are not documented. Contact support for current guidance on those topics.

## Billing Cycle and Renewal

The selected monthly or annual term determines the billing cycle. Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled. Exact charge timing, renewal notices, and billing-cycle date changes are not documented.

The approved product information does not define how to request an upgrade or downgrade, when a plan change takes effect, whether charges are prorated, or how a downgrade affects usage above the new plan's limits. ## Access After Cancellation

After cancellation, customers retain access until the end of their current billing period. Access does not end immediately when cancellation is requested. Exact cancellation steps are covered in the Cancellation and Refund Policy; where that document identifies an undocumented procedure, contact support for current instructions.
