# Cancellation and Refund Policy

TaskFlow is a fictional demonstration SaaS product. This policy explains the approved cancellation and refund rules for monthly and annual subscriptions.

## Cancel a Subscription

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled. The exact account screens, cancellation controls, required notice period, and role allowed to cancel are not documented in the approved product information. Contact TaskFlow support for the current cancellation procedure.

Do not assume that deleting a workspace, stopping product use, or disconnecting an integration cancels a subscription; no such behavior is documented.

## Access After Cancellation

Customers retain access until the end of the current billing period after cancellation. Cancellation does not remove access immediately. Behavior after the billing period ends, including data access or retention, is not documented.

## First-Payment Refund Eligibility

A first-time paid subscription can receive a refund when the customer requests it within **7 calendar days of the first payment**. The approved policy does not define exceptions beyond the refund cases stated in this article.

## Renewal Payments

Subscription renewals are normally non-refundable. Monthly and annual subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled. The policy does not document a refund right for a standard renewal payment.

## Duplicate Charges

Duplicate payments are refundable. Submit a refund request through billing support so the duplicate payment can be reviewed.

## Confirmed Billing Errors

Confirmed billing errors are refundable. A charge must be confirmed as a billing error before this rule applies. The investigation method and processing timeline are not documented.

## Request a Refund

Refund requests must be submitted through **billing support**. Follow billing support's instructions for any information needed to review the request.

The approved policy does not define a support address, required form, response time, refund-processing time, or payment-return method. Contact support for current operational details and do not send passwords, authentication codes, or other credentials.

## Policy Summary

- First-time paid subscriptions may be refunded when requested within 7 calendar days of the first payment.
- Renewal payments are normally non-refundable.
- Duplicate payments are refundable.
- Confirmed billing errors are refundable.
- Refund requests are submitted through billing support.
- Customers retain access until the end of the current billing period after cancellation.
